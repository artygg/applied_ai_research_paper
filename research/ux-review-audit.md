## Appraisal safeguards

No exact total of all existing direct Duolingo studies is claimed. Journal publication, independence, sample size, outcome validity, and intervention specificity are separate properties. Reviews may reuse primary studies; their sample counts and effects are not added together. Broader AI reviews are not automatically treated as generative-AI reviews. A lack of verified exposure is uncertainty, not proof that participants never used a feature.

Confidence is retained as a legitimate subjective outcome, but not renamed proficiency. App engagement/retention is distinguished from language retention. Commercial efficiency and instructional quality are evaluated separately. Both promotional and critical interpretations are held to these limits.
