### Remaining limitations and exact next steps

These are limitations of evidential strength or further improvements, not undisclosed failures of the mandatory form checks.

1. **Moderate: retrospective selection cannot be made historically reproducible by polishing the prose.** Lines 90–109 and 345–347 acknowledge missing search/exclusion history and selection effects. The exact next improvement would be a *new, dated, bounded update search* with saved query results and explicit decisions, followed by a reported comparison with the current selection. Do not invent old screening decisions or describe the current collection as exhaustive. No such new search is required by the supplied course rubric to make this focused review assessable.
2. **Moderate: several source appraisals remain access-limited.** Table 2 and the register disclose this. To strengthen the paper further, obtain the full original texts and appendices for the abstract-limited studies and summaries, then revise only claims actually supported by the recovered methods. Until then, the current narrower uses are appropriate. The review cannot establish the absence of independent studies across the whole literature.
3. **Moderate source-quality caveat: Ouyang's published numerical reporting warrants caution.** The original Table 2's affective means/SDs with 40 participants per group imply an ordinary independent-groups *t* of approximately 3.62, rather than its printed 12.3. This is my arithmetic check, not a corrected reanalysis of raw data. The paper currently uses this source only as contextual study description, so its central effect argument does not inherit that numerical claim. Keep that restricted use; record the inconsistency in appraisal notes before using its effect estimates. [Original publisher text, Methods and Table 2](https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/download/7677/6089?inline=1).
4. **Minor: bibliography presentation can still be made cleaner.** Catalan's version displays as an unexplained “(2)” rather than a labelled version/preprint description, and some reference entries contain review-process notes such as “Abstract inspected.” These are traceability/presentation issues, not missing source identities. A future tidy-up should label the preprint version plainly and keep reviewer-access notes in the register. Do not remove necessary version identifiers or original publication information.
5. **Submission dependency: deliver the research supplement.** Lines 92–104 refer readers to the instrument outside the PDF. The paper, method supplement and CSV must travel together; including the named historical logs and inventory allows readers to inspect the reconstructed trail. A lone PDF would not provide the whole instrument on which the Method section relies. The implementing agent has specified a package containing those supporting records; actual course submission remains the authors' responsibility.

## Limits of this assessment

I read the controlling supplied requirements and assessed every listed criterion. I independently checked consequential primary claims and metadata, arithmetic for visual proportions, citation/register coverage, the register's inventory correspondences, and rendered page contact sheets. I did not read every page of every cited source, reproduce primary-study analyses, inspect private product evaluations, determine hidden company priorities, perform an exhaustive literature search, certify originality/plagiarism clearance, or verify the authors' course-process compliance. Search indexing, mutable webpages and restricted downloads introduce their own limits.

The conclusion of **Sufficient** concerns this final manuscript and its supporting research records. It should not be converted into a guaranteed lecturer result or an unsupported ≥8.5 judgement. The remaining work needed for submission is chiefly the authors' course-process completion and responsibility for the evidence; higher evidential confidence would require further research access or new documented retrieval, not stronger wording.
