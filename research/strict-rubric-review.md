## Findings and exact revision targets

### 1. Major: the internal-optimisation conclusion is not established

**Where:** [RQ3 conclusion](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:315), [main conclusion](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:327), and the final sentence of [the company-reporting paragraph](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:312). Affects A consistency, B4 substantiation, and B5 consistency/validity.

The paper establishes what selected public documents report. It does not observe the full set of internal objectives, experiment guardrails, decision weights, or unpublished educational evaluations. The notification paper establishes an objective for one pre-generative mechanism, not for every generative feature. The company also explicitly presents teaching improvement as a priority in its [Q1 2026 shareholder letter, printed p. 4](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/ac220d7c-e313-4049-b309-1095fd24a86f). Neither accepting nor dismissing that statement establishes an internal hierarchy of objectives.

**Smallest effective revision:** confine the answer to the inspected public record. Suggested replacement: “In the inspected public record, production efficiency and activity are documented more consistently than independent, long-term learner benefits. This review cannot determine how Duolingo internally weights these objectives.” Apply the same qualification in Results and Conclusion. Alternatively, retain the stronger question but obtain evidence that can actually answer it. Adding another investor quote alone would not close this gap.

### 2. Major: source selection cannot be reconstructed

**Where:** [sample](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:87), [data collection](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:90), and [instruments/analysis](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:95). Affects all B3 items, especially data collection.

The declared 222-record collection and 45 cited keys are real counts in the repository. What is missing is the decision trail: which sources came from that collection, which were retrieved later, why each was included, what was excluded, and how ambiguous exposure/access cases were handled. The existing `focused-evidence.csv` contains 13 evidence records from a previous revision; it is not a complete current 45-source register. The later source log helps, but the paper does not direct its reader to a complete current instrument.

**Smallest effective revision:** supply a retrospective, clearly labelled source register covering the current citations, with source identifier, RQ relevance, inclusion rationale, access level, source locator, and appraisal decisions. Describe the actual retrieval routes and queries that are recorded. Distinguish historical searches from searches that can be documented now. Do not invent searches, exclusions, duplicate counts, or a PRISMA screening history; this is a focused review, and PRISMA is not required by the module documents.

### 3. Major: “enrichment” changes meaning across the argument

**Where:** [definition in RQ3](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:241), [RQ3 answer](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:315), and [conclusion](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:327). Affects A consistency, B4 consistency, and B5.

Enrichment is initially described as new support judged by learner outcomes. Later, the existence of additional features is treated as evidence that enrichment is “real,” even though learner benefits are described as thinly evaluated. Increased capability and demonstrated educational benefit are different propositions.

**Smallest effective revision:** define enrichment of available practice separately from improvement in learner outcomes, and maintain that distinction in the conclusion. State that the first is documented while the second remains conditional and unevenly supported. No extra experiment is needed to make this wording accurate.

### 4. Major: the habit/dependence argument treats interpretation as measured impact

**Where:** [habit-mechanism paragraph](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:308) and [autonomy paragraph](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:310). Affects B4 substantiation/depth and B5 validity.

The full [Maaß paper](https://www.wr-publishing.org/index.php/ijmat/article/view/908) was obtained for this review. Its method is observation of the iOS app over eight weeks, from December 2025 to January 2026, interpreted through habit literature. It does not report a Duolingo learner cohort tested for dependence or a longitudinal comparison of autonomous learning with continued app use. Part of its dependency argument draws on earlier research involving another app.

It is acceptable to report Maaß's interpretation as an interpretation. The paper's own conclusion that continued use is “the more reliably produced result” implies an outcome comparison this source cannot supply.

**Smallest effective revision:** describe the study as an observational design analysis, explicitly label the dependency interpretation, and replace the comparative outcome claim with a research question or hypothesis. Do not equate a plausible design mechanism with demonstrated harm.

### 5. Major: confidence, willingness to communicate, and change are not interchangeable

**Where:** [outcome definitions](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:169), [cross-study comparison](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:196), and [RQ2 conclusion](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:325). Affects B3 instruments, B4 consistency, and B5 validity.

Willingness to communicate concerns readiness/intention to communicate; self-efficacy concerns perceived capability for a task. A neutral post-use confidence response does not, by itself, estimate change in confidence. The detailed study paragraph already distinguishes stable willingness to communicate from neutral confidence perceptions, but “without a gain in confidence” and the claimed “mirror image” simplify this too far.

**Smallest effective revision:** retain separate labels for task-specific self-efficacy, willingness to communicate, post-use confidence perceptions, behavioural participation, and speaking performance. Specify which was measured before and after exposure. Suggested conclusion wording: “The Japanese study reported a speaking advantage, stable willingness to communicate, and neutral post-use confidence perceptions.” Do not infer equivalence from a non-significant result.

### 6. Moderate: the quantitative synthesis needs a clearer interpretation, not more numbers

**Where:** [field-level meta-analyses](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:174). Affects B4 depth and source quality.

The effect of 0.576 has a confidence interval but no named effect-size metric. The paper does better with the separate between/within-group estimates from Lee and Lee. Readers also need to understand why a heterogeneous mixture of classroom interventions cannot specify the expected benefit of a self-directed Duolingo feature. The text recognises this, but the positive moderators are still described as favourable to Duolingo without a product-level test.

**Smallest effective revision:** verify the metric, outcome, comparator basis, and bias caveats from the original synthesis. Report only what is inspectable. Explain that moderator patterns generate hypotheses; they do not validate a transfer to this app. For the Japanese speaking result, add magnitude/uncertainty only if the relevant original analysis can be inspected; otherwise retain a clearly attributed qualitative description. Do not reconstruct an effect from Figure 2's participation counts.

### 7. Moderate: the methodological contribution and scholarly gap should be explicit

**Where:** [objective and questions](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:62) and Section 2. Affects B2 logical connection and the introduction slides' requirement to identify a gap.

The paper contains an original contribution: it separates product changes, direct learner evidence, adjacent research, and company production metrics. The introduction does not state that contribution as a precise gap in prior research.

**Smallest effective revision:** add one evidence-qualified sentence stating that this review connects dated product changes with the specificity and limitations of the inspected learner-outcome evidence. Avoid claiming to be the first review unless that priority claim has been established. One or two sourced sentences explaining what a language model generates and how this differs from adaptive selection would also address the slides' “how it works” emphasis; Figure 1 provides a useful anchor.

### 8. Moderate: limitations should change the conclusion and recommendations

**Where:** [reliability and validity](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:334) and [recommendations](/Users/artyomgrishayev/GolandProjects/applied_ai_research_paper/main.tex:343). Affects B5 reliability, validity, recommendations, and usability.

The limitations are correctly named but largely listed. Explain their consequences: purposive selection can shape the apparent evidence asymmetry; company summaries may overlap; restricted access prevents complete appraisal; adherent analysis samples may favour those who tolerate the intervention. “No evidence in this review” must remain distinguishable from “no evidence exists.” The general statement that benefits “have not yet been publicly demonstrated” also needs to acknowledge the limited positive findings already reviewed.

**Smallest effective revision:** connect each major limitation to the specific claim it weakens. Prioritise two or three recommendations with an actor, an outcome, and an evaluation approach. For example, independent feature-version comparisons should report speaking transfer and validated self-report outcomes separately, with follow-up and non-completers accounted for. These are proposals, not completed work.

## Recommended revision order

1. Constrain the optimisation and enrichment conclusions to what the review can establish; reconcile Results, Conclusion, and validity discussion.
2. Make the current source-selection and appraisal process traceable without inventing retrospective procedures.
3. Separate the outcome constructs and correct the habit/dependence inference.
4. Clarify the scholarly gap, effect-size interpretation, and prioritised recommendations.
5. Obtain the required human/lecturer process evidence and perform the final source check.
6. Rebuild after substantive edits and recheck the 8–10-page body limit and figure/table references.

The visual work is complete. The remaining weaknesses require better methodological reporting and more restrained conclusions, rather than additional decorative images or unsupported numerical charts.
